## 6. Internal linking strategy

Internal links are the mechanism that moves a reader from informational to commercial intent:

1. **Service ↔ project:** every service page lists `relatedProjects` (project slugs as evidence); every project page links back to its owning `service` slug.
2. **Service ↔ article:** every service page lists `relatedArticles` (further reading); every article links to its `relatedServices` — the article-to-service funnel above.
3. **Article ↔ article:** `relatedArticles` on each article keeps readers inside the topic cluster.
4. **Breadcrumbs:** visible breadcrumbs on every detail page (Home → Section → Page), mirrored by `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.
5. **Indexes:** every index page links to all of its children; the header's grouped services dropdown exposes all 10 services site-wide; the footer repeats the main sections.
6. **Anchors:** contextual WhatsApp CTAs per page — the anchor text describes the page topic, never a generic "click here".

## 7. Rules

- One primary keyword per page — never two pages competing for the same head term.
- Keywords appear in title, H1, and first paragraph where natural; **no keyword stuffing**; copy is written for humans first.
- Long-tails (questions, tool names, repo names) are covered by articles and project pages, not by extra service pages.
- No fabricated search-volume claims, no fabricated rankings, no fake reviews — consistent with the site's honesty rules.
